When to Use Polygraph Testing

👤

Pre-Employment Screening

Verify the honesty and integrity of candidates before hiring — especially for positions involving finances, access to assets, or sensitive information.

🔎

Theft & Fraud Investigations

Investigate specific incidents of theft, fraud, or financial misconduct to identify individuals involved and support disciplinary proceedings.

⚖️

Workplace Misconduct

Resolve conflicting accounts of incidents by using polygraph results as supporting evidence alongside other investigation findings.

Important Legal Note on Polygraph Testing in South Africa

Employees cannot be compelled to undergo polygraph testing without written consent. Refusal alone cannot be used as proof of guilt. NGWS ensures every test is conducted lawfully and professionally in line with South African labour law.
