The Process

The CCMA Process Explained in Plain Language

1

Referral Received

Employee files a case — you receive notification

2

Conciliation

Both parties attempt to reach a settlement

3

Outcome

Settled or certificate of non-resolution issued

4

Arbitration

If unresolved, case goes to arbitration hearing

5

Award Issued

Commissioner issues a binding arbitration award
