Unfair dismissal is one of the most common disputes referred to the CCMA in South Africa. Understanding the difference between substantive and procedural fairness is critical for any employer.

## What Is Unfair Dismissal?

A dismissal is unfair if the employer cannot show that it was for a fair reason (substantive fairness) AND that a fair procedure was followed (procedural fairness).

## Substantive Fairness

Substantive fairness asks: was there a valid reason to dismiss? Valid reasons include misconduct, incapacity, or operational requirements (retrenchment).

## Procedural Fairness

Procedural fairness asks: was the correct procedure followed? This includes giving proper notice of the hearing, allowing the employee to state their case, and providing a written outcome.
